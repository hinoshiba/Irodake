Irodake is a menu bar and windowed macOS utility that applies selective grayscale using ScreenCaptureKit and a click-through Metal overlay.

No account or network connection is required.

Review steps:
1. Launch Irodake and complete the three onboarding screens.
2. Allow Screen Recording when requested. If macOS asks for a restart, reopen Irodake.
3. Keep “Keep Color” selected, add a fixed region by dragging, and turn the effect on. The display becomes grayscale while the selected rectangle remains in color.
4. Select “Gray Selected” to verify the inverse effect.
5. Use Option-Command-G for master On/Off and hold Option-Command-C to temporarily reveal full color.

The Screen Recording permission is required only to process frames locally. Irodake does not record to a file, capture audio, transmit data, or use private APIs. Protected/DRM content may appear blank or frozen because the app does not bypass capture protection.
