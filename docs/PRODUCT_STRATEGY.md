# Product Strategy

## Positioning

Irodake is not a generic display filter. It allocates color intentionally:

> 色は、必要な場所だけ。 / Color only where it matters.

Brand pronunciation is **ee-roh-dah-keh** / イロダケ. Keep the product name unlocalized as `Irodake`; explain the function in localized subtitles rather than appending generic keywords to the brand:

- Japanese subtitle: `部分カラー・画面グレースケール`
- English subtitle: `Selective Screen Grayscale`

The product model is “base display + opposite-effect spots,” presented as two simple modes:

- Keep Color: gray the display and leave selected spots in color.
- Gray Selected: keep the display in color and gray selected spots.

Avoid medical claims. Use “reduce visual stimulation” and “create a calmer workspace,” not claims to treat eye strain, sleep, ADHD, addiction, or another condition.

## Primary audience

1. knowledge workers and students reducing visual distraction;
2. designers/developers who usually prefer grayscale but need accurate status, chart, photo, or design color on demand;
3. multi-display users who want focus without dimming or blur.

## Launch target

“#1” must be measurable rather than promised. Proposed first target:

- Japan Mac App Store;
- paid Utilities category;
- daily rank #1;
- launch price around the current local equivalent of US$4.99, reviewed at launch.

The product cannot guarantee rank. Track first-effect success, 7-day retention, color-peek use, crash-free sessions, Energy Impact complaints, rating ≥4.7, and zero stuck-overlay incidents.

## MVP differentiators

- bidirectional grayscale/color spot model;
- hold-to-reveal Color Peek;
- fixed rectangles and tracked window rectangles;
- instant menu bar master switch;
- native, local, no account, no telemetry, open source;
- clear permission and DRM/overlap limitations.

## Business model

- MIT source and free self-builds;
- one-time paid official Mac App Store binary;
- direct signed build only after Store launch and operational maturity;
- no subscription for the local single-purpose core;
- official purchase value: trusted signature, easy delivery/update, QA, support, and legally cleared brand.

## App Store page

Primary category: Utilities. Secondary: Productivity.

Screenshot sequence:

1. mostly grayscale desktop with one vivid first-party demo window — “色は、必要な場所だけ。”
2. tracked window rectangle — “作業中の場所に、色がついてくる。”
3. drag region selection;
4. inverse mode;
5. menu bar ON/OFF;
6. Color Peek;
7. privacy: “メモリ上で処理。保存しない。送信しない。”
8. multiple displays.

Never show third-party logos, copyrighted photos, personal information, or behavior the release build does not reproduce.

## Roadmap after quality gates

- normalized display-specific regions;
- optional frontmost-window follow mode;
- cursor color lens;
- schedule and temporary pause;
- Focus / Shortcuts / App Intents;
- per-display presets;
- HDR color path;
- local-only usage insight.

Do not add accounts, cloud sync, AI classification, complex rules, or analytics before the core overlay is measurably stable and efficient.
